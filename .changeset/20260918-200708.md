`bb pr list` gains `--limit <n>` (default: 50), matching `gh pr list --limit`. Previously the command had no way to cap or raise the number of results — it silently stopped at an undocumented, hardcoded ceiling with no flag to change it. A non-positive or non-numeric value is rejected with `--limit must be a positive number, got '<value>'`.
